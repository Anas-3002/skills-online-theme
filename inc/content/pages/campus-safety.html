Campus safety

Conduct, platform safety and how to report a concern.

POLICY

Our commitment

Skills Online is a remote-first institute. "Campus" here means the learner portal, the cohort channels, the sandbox environment and any in-person workshop we run. The same standard of safety applies to all of them.

Community safety

  • A published code of conduct, accepted at enrolment, covering harassment, discrimination and professional conduct in shared spaces.
  • A named programme lead per cohort who is accountable for conduct issues, with a reporting route that does not require confronting the person involved.
  • Reports are acknowledged within one business day. Outcomes range from a documented warning to removal from the programme; serious allegations are escalated outside the programme team.

Platform and data safety

  • Sandbox environments are isolated per learner, with no access to other learners' repositories or to production systems.
  • Learner data handling, retention and your rights are described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Credentials are never shared between learners. Suspected account compromise should be reported immediately so sessions can be revoked.

In-person workshops

  • Venues are assessed for accessibility and emergency procedures before booking, and the assessment is documented.
  • Workshops are alcohol-free working sessions, and a named organiser is present for the duration.
  • Accessibility requirements collected at registration are passed to the venue in advance; tell us if something was missed and we will fix it.

Reporting

Send safety reports to admissions using the contact form and mark them "Safety". If a report concerns a member of the programme team, say so explicitly and it will be handled by someone outside that team.
