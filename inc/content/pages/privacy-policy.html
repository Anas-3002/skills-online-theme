Overview & Information We Collect

When you enroll in our software engineering, applied AI, or infrastructure tracks, Skills Online collects strictly structured data required to deliver high-yield educational programming, monitor syllabus completion, and issue verifiably backed credentials.

Legal name, primary contact address, and single-sign-on authorizations via GitHub, GitLab, or Google OAuth. We never solicit or store third-party account credentials.

1-on-1 Mentorship & Video Call Recording Policies

Direct technical mentorship is vital to mastering modern engineering stacks. Our platform utilizes WebRTC-encrypted peer channels for video conferences, pair programming, and mock architectural interviews.

videocam

Explicit Opt-In Audio/Video Recording

Sessions are recorded only when both the learner and mentor explicitly select the recording toggle. If initiated, an audible chime and permanent visual banner alert all attendees. Recordings automatically purge after 90 days unless archived into your personal portfolio locker.

All mentors undergo exhaustive background screening and execute binding bilateral confidentiality agreements. Mentors are contractually precluded from repurposing, disclosing, or reproducing any proprietary designs or conversational details exchanged during private office hours.
