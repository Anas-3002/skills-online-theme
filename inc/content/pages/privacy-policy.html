COLLECTION BOUNDARIES

Overview & Information We Collect

When you enroll in our software engineering, applied AI, or infrastructure tracks, Skills Online collects strictly structured data required to deliver high-yield educational programming, monitor syllabus completion, and issue verifiably backed credentials.

badge

Account Identity & Auth

Legal name, primary contact address, and single-sign-on authorizations via GitHub, GitLab, or Google OAuth. We never solicit or store third-party account credentials.

credit_card

Billing & Payment Data

Tuition is invoiced by Skills Online. This site does not store card numbers. Skills Online servers never process or persist raw credit card PANs.

terminal

Learning Telemetry

Unit test pass rates, IDE execution latency, quiz attempt durations, and peer code review participation. This telemetry guides individual syllabus remediation.

device_hub

Network & Device State

IP addresses (masked upon intake), user agent strings, and session nonces used strictly for account take-over prevention and automated DDoS mitigation.

04
COLLABORATION ETHICS

1-on-1 Mentorship & Video Call Recording Policies

Direct technical mentorship is vital to mastering modern engineering stacks. Mentor sessions run on the meeting tool your cohort uses. Recordings are not published without your consent.

videocam

Explicit Opt-In Audio/Video Recording

Sessions are recorded only when both the learner and mentor explicitly select the recording toggle. If initiated, an audible chime and permanent visual banner alert all attendees. Session recordings, where a cohort keeps them at all, are retained only as long as the cohort needs them and are deleted on request.

gavel

Staff+ Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)

All mentors undergo exhaustive background screening and execute binding bilateral confidentiality agreements. Mentors are contractually precluded from repurposing, disclosing, or reproducing any proprietary designs or conversational details exchanged during private office hours.

Cookies, Telemetry & Student Rights

TRACKING BOUNDARIES

Zero Advertising Pixels

We do not execute third-party ad retargeting pixels (e.g., Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel) on authenticated student workspaces. Cookies are limited to essential auth state and load balancing tokens.

AUTONOMOUS CONTROL

GDPR / CCPA Direct Entitlements

Under California and European frameworks, you hold the right to Know, Correct, Delete, and Port. You can request a machine-readable export of the data we hold about you, and we provide it once we have verified the request.
