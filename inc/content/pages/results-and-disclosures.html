Results & disclosures

How to read the figures on this site, what is verified, and what is illustrative.

EFFECTIVE FROM PUBLICATION

This page explains how the numbers on this website should be read. It exists because education marketing is full of figures that are technically true and practically misleading, and we would rather be plain about ours.

What we publish as fact

  • Programme design. Cohort length, module count, mentor cadence, class size, the number of reviewed deliverables, the technologies taught. These are things we control and can be checked against the delivered curriculum.
  • Curriculum content. The module list on each track page is the published curriculum for the current cohort. When it changes, it changes there first.
  • Policies. The refund window, deferral policy, tuition, instalment terms and scholarship process are commitments, not estimates.

What is illustrative, not verified

  • Salary and package ranges. Where a figure such as a typical package appears, it describes the range of roles graduates apply for and the bands reported by hiring partners in that discipline. It is not an average of verified graduate outcomes and it is not a promise about your result.
  • Placement rates and time-to-hire. Any such figure shown on this site is a programme target or an internally tracked rate that has not been independently audited. Treat it as a direction of travel, not a guarantee.
  • Ratings and review counts. Aggregated satisfaction scores reflect the cohort surveys we run at the end of each module. They are self-reported and not independently audited.
  • Alumni stories. The cards on the reviews page are illustrative composites — descriptions of the shape of journeys our mentors see, written to show the kind of work involved. They are not attributed to named, verified individuals.
  • Company names. Where employer names appear (for example as places alumni have moved to), they describe destinations of past cohorts and do not imply that those companies endorse, sponsor or are affiliated with Skills Online.

Guarantees, precisely

The 14-day refund is unconditional within its window: request it and it is paid, no justification required. Any placement commitment we make in writing for a specific programme is governed by the terms stated on that programme's page, including the completion conditions (attendance, submitted work, application volume) that must be met for it to apply. A guarantee you cannot trigger is not a guarantee, so those conditions are published rather than buried.

How to check us

On an admissions call you can ask for: the curriculum as delivered for the current cohort, the mentor roster, an anonymised sample of reviewed student work, and our cohort completion rate. We will share them. If a figure you have seen on this site is not covered here, ask us to source it — and if we cannot, we will correct the page.
